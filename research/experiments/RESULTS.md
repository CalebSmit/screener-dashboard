**How some screens are built here** (`construction_note`):

- **only-size** - Ranked within sector, as the engine ranks every metric (factor_engine.compute_sector_percentiles): the screen favours companies that are small for their sector. Fama & French (1992) sort the whole market by size.
- **piotroski-only** - Ranked within sector, as the engine ranks every metric (factor_engine.compute_sector_percentiles): the F-score is compared with sector peers. Piotroski (2000) applies it across the market, among high book-to-market firms.
- **profitability-and-value** - Ranked within sector, as the engine ranks every metric (factor_engine.compute_sector_percentiles). The value half is earnings yield, where Novy-Marx (2013) uses book-to-market; his hedge comes from profitability's negative correlation with book-to-market. Here earnings yield and gross profit to assets have an average monthly within-sector rank correlation of +0.045 over 176 months (research/measurements/2026-10-16-stocks-ranked-per-config.json), so the screen does not carry that hedge.
- **fcf-yield-only** - Ranked within sector, as the engine ranks every metric (factor_engine.compute_sector_percentiles): free-cash-flow yield against sector peers, which is how the screener's Valuation category uses it.
- **momentum-12-1-only** - Ranked within sector, as the engine ranks every metric (factor_engine.compute_sector_percentiles): a stock's 12-1 month return against its sector peers, which removes the industry component of momentum (Moskowitz & Grinblatt 1999). Jegadeesh & Titman (1993) rank across the whole market.
- **low-volatility-only** - Ranked within sector, as the engine ranks every metric (factor_engine.compute_sector_percentiles): volatility against sector peers. Ang et al. (2006) and Frazzini & Pedersen (2014) sort across the whole market.
- **52-week-high** - Ranked within sector, as the engine ranks every metric (factor_engine.compute_sector_percentiles): nearness to the 52-week high against sector peers. George & Hwang (2004) rank across the whole market.
